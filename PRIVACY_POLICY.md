# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-06-06

Zaragoza Tarjeta Bus, developed by Viktor Shchelochkov, does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. It contains no analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or advertising, makes no network requests, and sends nothing to any server or any third party. The app uses NFC solely for its on-device functionality; this data never leaves your device.
